These maps visualize rent burden in San Francisco, alongside point locations of affordable housing in the city. This was prepared as part of a term paper for the course Principles of Housing at Rutgers in 2025.

<img src="image/MAP4_Rentburden_Principles of Housing.png" alt="Rent Burden in San Francisco">

San Francisco faces acute housing affordability issues. More than half of the Southern and Eastern parts of the city are rent burdened, which means they spend more than 30% of their income on housing. This affects neighborhoods Bayview, Hunters Point, Portola, Visitacion Valley and the Excelsior the most, with sections with 80% of their residents rent burdened. 

<img src="image/MAP3_SF_AH.png" alt="Affordable Housing in San Francisco">

Affordable housing developments are concentrated in dense neighborhoods in the Northeastern quadrant of the city, away from areas with the most rent burden: Mission Bay, South of Market, the Tenderloin. 

<img src="image/MAP5_RentBurdenAffordableHousing_Principles of Housing.png" alt="Rent Burden and Affordable Housing in San Francisco">
<br>

Affordable housing developments surround Market Street, and may be contributing to the lower percentages of rent burdened households in those tracts. However, the existing housing has not adequately addressed the housing affordability crisis, highlighting a need for expanded affordable housing in the city.
