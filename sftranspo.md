This assignment explores the impact of ridesharing on public transport using analyses from census data, SF transit agencies, and ridesharing companies between 2011 and 2022. It was a group assignment for the course "Urban Data Analytics" taken in the summer of 2023. My project partners were Royce Chan and Calvin Carter. The analysis explores commuting patterns, income, accessibility, and traffic congestion to gain insights on why people choose rideshare over public transport. We found that the time-efficiency of rideshare was a large factor. I prepared the analyses on per capita income as well as comparing the costs of public transit and rideshare. My data was sourced from the American Community Survey, government websites, and rideshare companies.
