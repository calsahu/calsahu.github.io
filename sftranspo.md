[Assignment researching impact of ridesharing on public transportation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16mHV6zUrckrcL0oaMdeLqeIdWNRQVaWXkU-9goHb6y0/edit?usp=sharing) 
<br>
This assignment explores the impact of ridesharing on public transport using analyses from census data, SF transit agencies, and ridesharing companies. The analysis explores commuting patterns, income, accessibility, and traffic congestion to gain insights on why people choose rideshare over public transport. We found that the time-efficiency of rideshare was a large factor. I prepared the analyses on per capita income as well as comparing the costs of public transit and ride.
