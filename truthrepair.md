I was a researcher for the Truth and Repair project in the Summer of 2026, studying the health and wellbeing of historically disadvantaged communities in New Jersey. I worked on mapping historic inequities across New Jersey, with a particular focus on food insecurity. My maps highlight congruences between urban renewal and redlining in New Jersey and present-day disparities. My sources included the American Community Survey, the Plan4HealthNJ tool, the National Neighborhood Data Archive (NaNDA), USDA’s SNAP Retailer Data, redlining and urban renewal data from the University of Richmond Digital Scholarship Lab.    

You can see the whole project 
[here](https://trnj.org/)

## New Jersey
We focused on 7 municipalities across the state: New Brunswick, Trenton, Atlantic City, Camden, Jersey City, Newark, and Plainfield. Observing race data from the ACS, it is evident that there is a diagonal pattern of minority majority areas which are also sites of historic urban renewal and displacement.
